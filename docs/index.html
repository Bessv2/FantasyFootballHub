Overview

Standings

Ordered by record, then total points scored — the same tiebreak ESPN uses for this league. Luck is wins above or below what the scores alone deserved. Efficiency is how much of each roster's possible points actually got started.

Teams

Every manager, with the numbers that separate good teams from lucky ones.

My Team

Draft

Pick value is measured inside the draft itself: every drafted player is ranked by the points they actually scored, then compared to where they were taken. Positive value means the player outperformed the cost of the pick.

Trades & Transactions

Every completed trade, plus waiver and free-agent activity.

Side Prizes

Season awards computed from the box scores. These update every time the data is refreshed.
